# Key Findings Summary

## Project Title
Logistics Optimization for Cost-Efficient E-Commerce Distribution Decisions

## Core Result
The baseline nearest-center policy achieved a lower weighted average distance of 294.70 km, but it violated capacity constraints at 4 fulfillment centers and generated 26,201 units of excess demand.

The optimized capacity-constrained allocation model increased the weighted average distance to 345.11 km, but eliminated all capacity violations and reduced total excess demand to 0.

## Baseline vs Optimized Policy
- Baseline total transportation cost: 15,115,364.07
- Optimized total transportation cost: 17,701,059.42
- Cost change vs baseline: 17.11%
- Baseline weighted average distance: 294.70 km
- Optimized weighted average distance: 345.11 km
- Baseline capacity violated centers: 4
- Optimized capacity violated centers: 0
- Baseline total excess demand: 26,201
- Optimized total excess demand: 0

## Sensitivity Analysis
When the capacity buffer increased from 1.00 to 1.30, total transportation cost decreased from 18,415,335.87 to 16,345,438.15, and weighted average distance decreased from 359.04 km to 318.68 km.

This suggests that additional fulfillment capacity improves routing flexibility and reduces logistics cost.
